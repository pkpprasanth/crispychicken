FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our chicken menu, prices, WhatsApp ordering, and outlet location in Thiruvannamalai.

🍗 What chicken items does Crispy Chicken Spot serve?

We serve our signature Crispy Chicken Leg (₹80), 16-piece Chicken Popcorn (₹100), Chicken Lollipops (₹100), Chicken Wings (₹100), bone-in Crispy Chicken cuts (₹100), along with chicken burgers, steamed & fried momos, wraps, pizza, and chilled craft mojitos.

🍿 How much does chicken popcorn cost?

Our crunchy 16-piece Chicken Popcorn costs exactly ₹100. It is made from 100% boneless chicken cubes with authentic crispy batter and peri-peri seasoning dust.

🍔 How much does the chicken burger cost?

Our classic Chicken Burger is priced at ₹100, and our best-selling Chicken Cheese Burger layered with molten cheddar cheese and fresh vegetables is ₹120.

🥟 Does Crispy Chicken Spot offer chicken momos?

Yes! We offer handmade Steamed Chicken Momos (6 Pcs) for ₹100 and deep-fried Crunchy Chicken Momos (6 Pcs) for ₹100, both served with fiery red schezwan chutney.

💬 Can I order Crispy Chicken Spot through WhatsApp?

Yes, you can order directly through WhatsApp at +91 90876 49452. Tap any "WhatsApp Order" button on our website to send an auto-formatted message with your selected items.

🕒 What are the opening hours?

We are open daily from 11:00 AM to 03:00 AM (midnight/late-night active), making us Thiruvannamalai's go-to destination for daytime lunch, evening snacks, and late-night feasts.

📍 Where is Crispy Chicken Spot located?

Our shop is located at 252 Vellore Road, Vengikkal, Thiruvannamalai – 606604, Tamil Nadu (near the Food Street Hub). Google Maps navigation is available on this website.

🛵 Do you offer takeaway and food delivery?

Yes, we offer instant counter takeaway and fast bike delivery within a 5 KM radius of Vengikkal and Thiruvannamalai. Free delivery is automatically unlocked on orders above ₹300!
