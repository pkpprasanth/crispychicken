## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-octane, premium Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) delivery experience centered around craft fried chicken. The aesthetic rejects the playful, cheap fast-food tropes of bright yellow and flat primary reds, shifting instead into dark culinary luxury. The product identity balances the raw energy of street-level smoke and flame with the precision of an ultra-modern digital flagship.

The visual style unites **High-Contrast Dark Mode** with **Refined Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian canvas (#050505) creates a dramatic backdrop that makes golden, crispy textures and dynamic culinary photography stand out with appetite appeal.
- **Energy:** Fiery orange gradients, hot flame reds, and controlled neon amber glows evoke searing heat, fresh-from-the-fryer indulgence, and rapid delivery velocity.
- **Tactility:** Surfaces use subtle smoky glassmorphism, soft specular chrome highlights, and crisp micro-borders to maintain structural authority and premium polish.
- **Emotion:** Seductive, crave-inducing, energetic, and unapologetically bold.

## Colors

The palette relies on deep value contrasts between pure volcanic shadows and luminous thermal highlights.

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** 
  - `canvas-base`: `#050505` (Obsidian core)
  - `surface-recessed`: `#0A0A0A`
  - `surface-card`: `#121212` (Dark slate base)
  - `surface-elevated`: `#1E1E1E` (Active interactive layers)
  - `surface-floating`: `rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.75)` with 20px blur

- **Brand Thermals:**
  - `primary`: `#FF8A00` (Molten orange for key CTAs, pricing, and active selections)
  - `secondary`: `#FF9F1C` (Amber glow, used for highlights, badge fills, and gradient ramps)
  - `tertiary`: `#E53935` (Flame red, reserved for spice indicators, urgent deals, and critical statuses)
  - `accent-glow`: `rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.28)` (Soft radiance cast beneath interactive points)

- **Typography & Details:**
  - `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp bone white)
  - `text-secondary`: `#A1A1AA` (Cool muted slate)
  - `text-tertiary`: `#71717A` (Inactive metadata)
  - `border-subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - `border-glow`: `rgba(255, 159, 28, 0.35)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Epilogue** for display and headline expressions with **Space Grotesk** for functional UI copy, numbers, and culinary tags.

- **Epilogue (Headlines):** High-impact, heavy geometric proportions inject physical weight and swagger into product titles, spice ratings, and marketing punchlines. 
- **Space Grotesk (Body & Labels):** Provides an architectural, contemporary cadence that keeps tabular data, pricing, ingredient rosters, and modifier lists clean, hyper-legible, and modern.
- **Stylistic Directives:**
  - Headings (`display-hero`, `headline-xl`) use negative letter spacing to feel punchy and compact.
  - Category tags, spice metrics, and micro-labels leverage uppercase tracking (`label-caps`) to maintain legibility against dark slate surfaces.
  - Numerical pricing displays pair standard body weights with semi-bold currency glyphs for instant cart scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a flexible responsive grid built to showcase dense visual menus alongside high-impact hero products.

- **Grid System:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (16px), `gutter-mobile` (12px). Full-bleed horizontal scroll carousels for modifier categories and side items.
  - **Tablet (768px–1024px):** 8 columns, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Menu cards organize into dynamic 2-column food presentation blocks.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12 columns, `margin-desktop` (48px) with a max content container of `1440px`. Two-tier division: 8 columns dedicated to culinary browsing, 4 columns anchored to sticky cart, checkout, or ingredient deep-dives.

- **Spacing Cadence:**
  - Element packing uses an 8px base rhythm with tight internal card paddings (`space-md`) to ensure hero imagery dominates the viewport.
  - Section headers maintain generous lower separation (`space-lg`) before item grids, providing clear separation between combo sets, tenders, sides, and signature dipping sauces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered dark matter rather than traditional cast drop-shadows. The canvas shifts from total blackness to illuminated atmospheric layers:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Pure `#050505` creates maximum contrast for transparent imagery cutouts.
  - **Card Layer (`#121212`):** Surrounded by a hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` with no cast shadow in default resting states.
  - **Raised / Hovered Surfaces (`#1E1E1E`):** Lifted via an ambient orange-tinted backlight: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.18)`.
  - **Floating Cart & Navigation Drawers:** Frosted translucent backing `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a top-edge glass highlight `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, transparent 100%)`.

- **Neon Glows & Radiance:**
  - Hot items, "Extra Crispy" tags, and primary action buttons emit subtle radial blurs (`filter: drop-shadow(0 4px 20px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.4))`).
  - No harsh directional shadows; depth relies strictly on luminosity, surface lightness deltas, and glowing proximity effects.

## Shapes

The design system employs a structured, modern curvature (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem / 8px base):

- **Interactive Core:** Buttons, inputs, and modifier toggles use `rounded-md` (8px) to feel sharp and technical.
- **Card Containers:** Product tiles and modal sheets utilize `rounded-lg` (16px) and `rounded-xl` (24px) to gently enclose organic food shapes without feeling overly bubbly or childish.
- **Food Badges & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular product boxes and convey quick readability.
- **Image Treatment:** Dish presentation containers feature clipped corners or soft curved vignettes that merge seamlessly into dark backgrounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Order / Add to Cart):** Vibrant gradient background from `#FF8A00` to `#FF9F1C`. Bold dark text (`#050505`), rounded to 8px. Casts an ambient amber glow (`0 8px 24px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.35)`). Hover increases brightness and expands glow spread.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. White text. On hover, background shifts to `#1E1E1E` with border color shifting to `#FF8A00`.
- **Flame Accent Button:** Reserved for spice upgrades and instant flash drops. Solid `#E53935` with white typography and a subtle crimson glow.

### Cards & Menu Tiles
- Built on `#121212` with a `1px` outer border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- Image containers sit at the top of the card with an atmospheric radial gradient (`rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.08)`) centered directly behind the crispy chicken asset to highlight golden crags and seasoning glazes.
- Card footers feature prominent pricing in `Epilogue` Bold, aligned alongside an embedded quick-add button.

### Chips & Spice Gauges
- **Category Filter Chips:** Resting state is `#18181B` surface with muted text. Active state transitions to `#FF8A00` text with an amber-tinted border (`#FF8A00`) and subtle `rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.12)` background tint.
- **Heat Meter Indicator:** Custom pill chip featuring stylized flame icons. Scales progressively from Mild (amber `#FF9F1C`) to Inferno (deep red `#E53935` with pulse animation).

### Input Fields & Modifiers
- **Inputs:** Dark slate surfaces (`#121212`), 1px subtle white borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`). Focus state transitions border to `#FF8A00` paired with an inner glow. Crisp white text with zinc placeholders.
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes (Sauce & Side Selection):** Custom circular and squircle controls. Checked state fills with `#FF8A00` containing an obsidian icon tick, delivering sharp, positive feedback.

### Sticky Cart Dock (Mobile & Desktop)
- Floats over the menu canvas with high-blur frosted glass (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.85)`). 
- Features an integrated real-time order breakdown, interactive heat level recap, and an edge-to-edge glowing checkout trigger.
